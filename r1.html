r1 - Mars Rover

Capability: Habitat Planter on Mars performs autonomous tasks such as digging small holes on the ground to plant seeds and water them, combining Mars soil with fertilizer. Rechargeable battery and power by unfoldable and foldable solar panels for Mars dust storms. Inspection camera, ultrasonic sensor to detect objects.

Size: Ultra lightweight & affordable optimized to be lighter than any other existing rover. This ultimately means the mission will be more affordable to you. It is capable of communicating long-range and traveling multiple kilometers across the lunar surface. Bluetooth module is used for communication wireless communication distance.
