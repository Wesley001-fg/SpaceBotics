Who are we

Spacebotics is a team of passionate engineers and scientists dedicated to advancing space robotics. We believe that the future of space exploration lies in the development of autonomous, low-cost robotic systems that can operate in the harsh environments of other planets.

WHAT WE DO:

Aiming to make space accessible to the world through specializing in autonomous affordable robots.

THE FACILITY:

Johannesburg South - Where all the software, hardware testing and development takes place.

Our Staging Facility

The facility in Johannesburg South serves as the baseline workshop environment where the core foundation builds and operates its lines of landers, planetary rovers, autonomous spacecraft navigation systems, and other cutting-edge space technologies.

Mission Control & Payload Tracking

Off-world hardware management is handled through a highly secure, real-time environment. Spacebotics payloads will be controlled directly from our Mission Control Center inside Rosebank International College, located at the Braamfontein Campus inside the 4th-floor computer lab.

Our engineering team has quick, uninterrupted communication access to track rovers, and launch commands from terminal when needed as they navigate on their missions.

We distribute our blueprints,software scripts, and hardware prototype to global space organizations entirely for free or through academic research grants.
