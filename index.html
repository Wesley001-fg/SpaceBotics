Space Technology

Spacebotics buildes robotic solutions that enable humanity's space exploration across the solar system. We design, build, and deploy low-cost robotic hardware.

As a non-profit, we engineer cutting-edge aerospace platforms. Our goal is to revolutionize robot space technology by creating robust rovers that will assist future missions on the international space agencies in Earth orbit, the Moon, Mars, and beyond.



Mars Planter

Our specialized Mars planter is engineered to execute fully autonomous agricultural and environmental preparation tasks. It actively navigates the landscape to dig precise seed holes, drop plant seeds, distribute water, and blend raw fertilizers.

Hardware specs: Built with an object-tracking ultrasonic sensor and an camera. The rover utilizes rechargeable batteries powered by solar energy, dust-resistant folding solar panels designed for extreme Martian storms.

Lightweight robot

We completely redesigned the rover chassis to eliminate unnecessary mass. By using lightweight components to be lighter. With the aim to lower total operational mission costs.

Range & Connectivity: Despite its weight, this machine can travel long distances, across challenging terrains. It maintains long-range wireless communication system by a Bluetooth module component.

Resource Discovery: Ice Drilling

Sustaining life away from Earth requires water. One of the rovers built features are heavy-duty mechanical drill and extract frozen ice samples, and photograph.

Hardware: Uses the signature Spacebotics dual-layer protection suite, combining obstacle-avoiding ultrasonic, high-resolution cameras, and unfoldable solar panels that will prevent the panels from being covered in dust.
