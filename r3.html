r3 - Drone

Capability: Fully autonomous drone that can fly up to 100 kilometers and 100 feet in the air, takes photographs, go to difficult areas where the robots and humans cannot. Communicates in real-time with the r robots for them to automatically adjust power, suspension to fit upcoming terrain. Rechargeable battery and power by unfoldable and foldable solar panels for Mars dust storms. Inspection camera, two blades.

Size: Slightly heavier than the r1 and r2. It is capable of communicating long-range and traveling multiple kilometers across with bluetooth module is used for communication wireless communication.

We distribute our blueprints,software scripts, and physical prototype to global space organizations entirely for free or through academic research grants.
